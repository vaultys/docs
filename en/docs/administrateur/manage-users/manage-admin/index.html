Manage Users

1. Users Page​

Then click on the Users tab to access the Users page.

2. Modify Rights​

You can directly assign administrator rights to your users by clicking on the switch in the Administrator column.

3. Deactivate a User​

Click on the switch in the Active column to deactivate a user.

An inactive user will not be able to log in to SmartLink.

4. Manage Shares​

Click on the button in the Share column to manage the folders and applications that the user has access to.

5. Actions​

Click on the button in the Actions column to display more options.

You can then access the user's profile, invite the user by email to join your SmartLink, or delete the user.

6. Onboard Directly​

You can onboard a user directly by clicking on the QR code. They will then need to scan the QR code that will appear on the screen using their VaultysID application. They will then be part of your SmartLink and will be able to connect to it.
